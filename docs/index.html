      Calibration

      When a forecaster says 70%, does it happen about 70% of the time?

      Settled forecasts are grouped into ten buckets by what was predicted (0–10%, 10–20%, and so on). Each dot shows a bucket’s average prediction (across) against how often those events happened (up). Bigger dots hold more questions.

      A perfectly calibrated forecaster sits on the dashed diagonal. Dots below it mean overconfidence in “Yes”; dots above it mean underconfidence. Small buckets bounce around a lot.
