Calibration

When a forecaster says 70%, does it happen about 70% of the time?

Every settled forecast goes into one of ten buckets by what was predicted (0–10%, 10–20%, and so on). Each dot shows the average prediction in a bucket (across) against how often those events actually happened (up). Bigger dots hold more questions.

A perfectly calibrated forecaster sits on the dashed diagonal. Dots below it mean overconfidence about “Yes” (things happened less often than predicted); dots above it mean underconfidence.

Buckets with only a few questions bounce around a lot, so don’t read much into them until there are dozens.
